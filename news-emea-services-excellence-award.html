YourCompass Wins Dynatrace EMEA Services Excellence Award

May 21, 2025

FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE

[Dubai, UAE] – [May 21, 2025] – YourCompass, a leading technology services provider dedicated to delivering exceptional business outcomes, is thrilled to announce its recognition as the recipient of the prestigious Dynatrace EMEA Services Excellence Award. This award acknowledges Your Compass's outstanding commitment to customer success, underpinned by strategic investments in skill development and service delivery excellence.

The Dynatrace Services Excellence Award is bestowed upon partners who demonstrate an exceptional dedication to advancing their capabilities and delivering measurable customer impact. Your Compass stood out for its high-performing services business, marked by a focus on hands-on learning, certifications, and a relentless commitment to customer satisfaction.

"We are very proud to receive the Dynatrace EMEA Services Excellence Award," said Mohamed Haroun, CEO at Your Compass. "This achievement is a testament to our team's hard work, our dedication to ongoing professional development, and our commitment to delivering exceptional results for our customers. We are committed to help organizations unlock opportunities and optimize their operations in today's fast-changing digital world."

Luis Porem, VP of EMEA Partner Sales at Dynatrace, applauded Your Compass's achievement, stating, "Your Compass has demonstrated exceptional commitment in driving customer success by consistently investing in building expertise and creating impactful solutions. Their ability to integrate these skills and deliver meaningful outcomes underlines their position as a valuable Dynatrace partner. Congratulations to the entire team at Your Compass."

Your Compass's dedication to excellence in the services business and its steadfast focus on skill enhancement and customer-centric solutions perfectly align with the values of the Dynatrace Partner Program. Through their efforts, Your Compass continues to elevate industry standards and empower businesses across EMEA to achieve their goals with confidence.

About Your Compass

YourCompass acts as a pivotal partner in the digital transformation journey of businesses, dedicated to instigating growth and enduring success in the contemporary digital era. It enables its clients to harness the robust capabilities of data and artificial intelligence, modernize core technology, adopt new technological advancements, optimize and automate operations, and nurture digital talent and culture.

YourCompass is a proficient digital solutions provider committed to guiding enterprises through their digital transformation journeys. By leveraging state-of-the-art technologies and building robust partnerships, Your Compass provides solutions that drive operational effectiveness and sustainable business advancement.
