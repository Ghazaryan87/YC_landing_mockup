The Foundation

Three pillars. By design, not by exception.

Security and governance aren't add-ons, they're engineered in from the first line of code.

PILLAR 01

Enterprise-First

Built for enterprise governance from day one: role-based access control, full audit logs of every action, human-in-the-loop approvals for destructive operations, and alignment with ISO 27001, SOC 2 Type II, and government standards, documentation provided on request.

Deploys inside your walls, Kubernetes for production, a single VM for POC, or fully air-gapped where required. Data residency and sovereignty by architecture: no documents, no embeddings, no queries ever leave your network without explicit authorisation.

Integrates with what you already run, by design, never rip-and-replace. Your back-end systems become the AI's hands and eyes.
