Where AI is today

Three stages of AI evolution — we build at Stage 3

Most organisations are mid-journey. Understanding where you are determines what you can build — and whether it will survive production.

STAGE 1Foundational

RAG & basic AI

  • Chat-based, single-turn interactions
  • Rigid, rule-driven, human-controlled
  • Ticket-oriented, no cross-system context
  • No memory between sessions
Typical capabilityFAQ chatbot, basic document search
STAGE 2Evolving

Intelligent agents

  • Reasoning-capable, dynamic workflows
  • Mostly human-supervised execution
  • Multi-step tasks within a single system
  • Limited cross-system awareness
Typical capabilityIncident triage, automated diagnostics
STAGE 3Where we build

Autonomous AI residents

  • Persistent memory and identity across sessions
  • Cross-system context from live telemetry
  • Detect → diagnose → remediate, autonomously
  • Governed autonomy — human supervisor, full audit trail
  • Continuously evaluated and cost-controlled in production
Typical capabilitySelf-healing operations, an AI workforce that runs alongside your team

Why it matters: jumping to Stage 3 without Stage-1 foundations fails; staying at Stage 1 leaves value on the table. We meet you where you are and engineer the path up.
